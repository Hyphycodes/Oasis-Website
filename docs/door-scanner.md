# The door — one page

Print this and keep it at the podium.

---

## Starting the night

1. Open **oasismexicankitchenbar.com/admin** on your phone and sign in.
2. Tap **Events → Door**, then **Scan**. The event running tonight is picked for you.
3. Tap **Start scanning** and allow the camera when your phone asks.
4. Tap **This phone** and name it — *Main door — Carlos's phone*. Every check-in
   you make is recorded under that name, which is how a disputed scan gets
   sorted out later.

The list of tonight's tickets downloads to your phone as soon as the page
opens. **After that the scanner works without signal.** The top of the screen
tells you: `87 / 120 checked in`, and `offline` or `3 to sync` when the wifi has
dropped.

## Scanning

Point the camera at the guest's code. The whole screen answers:

| Colour | Means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Green — Welcome in** | Valid, first time. | Let them in. |
| **Amber — Already scanned** | Somebody came in on this ticket already; the time is on screen. | Ask. Most are honest mistakes — a screenshot sent to a friend, or a double tap. A manager can tap **Let them in anyway**. |
| **Amber — Wrong event** | A real ticket, for a different night. | Not tonight's. Send them to a manager; do not treat them like a forgery. |
| **Red — Refunded** | Money was given back. | Not valid. A manager can sell them a door ticket. |
| **Red — Not valid here** | Cancelled ticket. | Not valid. Get a manager. |
| **Red — No such ticket** | Nothing matches that code. | Try **Search** before anything else — the code may just be unreadable. |

It buzzes and beeps too, so you can work without staring at the screen. **Sound**
in the top right turns that off.

**Light** in the bottom corner turns the phone's torch on, if it has one. Ask the
guest to turn their brightness up — a dim screen is the one thing the camera
struggles with.

## Their phone is dead

Tap **Search**. Type a name, an order number, or the last part of the code.
You will see everyone matching, whether they are already in, and — if you are a
manager — a **Let in** button.

Search works offline too, on names and order numbers. Email search needs signal.

Nobody should ever be left outside because their battery died.

## Typing a code

The box at the bottom takes a code by hand: `H7K4-M2Q9`. Use it when a screen is
cracked or the camera will not focus. Codes never contain the letters I, L, O or
U, so what looks like an O is a zero.

## Going offline

Nothing to do. Keep scanning. The screen says `offline`, and a count of scans
waiting to sync. When signal comes back they go up on their own, keeping the
time you actually scanned them — so the arrival times stay honest even if your
phone syncs after somebody else's.

If two doors scan the same ticket while both are offline, the **earlier** scan is
the one that counts, and the second shows up in the admin as scanned twice.

## Ending the night

Tap **End shift**. Anything still waiting is sent first, and the ticket list is
wiped off your phone. Do this every night: a phone that goes home with the door
list on it is the guest list nobody is guarding.

## What door staff cannot do

Not a mistake, not a bug — ask a manager:

- Let somebody in on a second scan (**Let them in anyway**)
- Let somebody in from Search
- See what anyone paid, or any customer's email
- Refund anything

## When something is really wrong

Fall back in this order:

1. **Search** by name or order number.
2. **Type the code** by hand.
3. If the whole system is down: write names on paper and let people in. The
   admin can add them afterwards. A queue outside is worse than a list you
   reconcile in the morning.

Anything strange — the same ticket at two doors, somebody insisting they paid —
is recorded. Every scan is logged, including the ones that failed.
