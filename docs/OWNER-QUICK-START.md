# Owner Quick Start

Everything you need to run the Oasis website yourself. Five minutes to read.

---

## 1. Logging in

Go to **yoursite.com/admin** and enter your email and password.

Bookmark it. Save the password in your phone's password manager.

Forgot it? Ask whoever set up your account — there is no self-service reset, deliberately.

---

## 2. Change today's hours

**Hours & closures** → set the times → **Save hours**.

Open past midnight? Set the closing time to the small hours: opens `10:00`, closes `01:00`. The
website works out that means the next day.

Closed all day? Tick the **Closed** box for that day.

> This is the most important thing on the whole site. Hours show in the footer of every page, on
> the Visit page, and in Google search results.

---

## 3. Put a special on the website

**Announcement bar** → type your message → tick **Show this on the website** → **Save changes**.

It appears as a coloured strip across the top of every page. A live preview shows exactly how it
will look before you save.

Keep it to one short sentence — it has to fit on a phone.

**Set an end date and it turns itself off.** Otherwise remember to switch it off when the special
ends. An expired promotion is worse than none.

---

## 4. Add an event

You don't need to. **Friday and Saturday repeat automatically** and the website always shows the
correct upcoming dates — you will never add a date by hand.

What you *can* change: **Events** → pick the night → set the status (sold out, cancelled, free
entry), the ticket price, or the ticket link → **Save**.

Cancelled nights stay on the website for two weeks so people who were planning to come find out
rather than turn up to nothing.

For a genuine one-off — a holiday party, a guest DJ — call your developer. It takes them minutes.

---

## 5. Update a menu item

**Menus** → find the dish → **Edit** → change it → **Save**.

- **Price:** numbers only. `16` or `16.50`. No dollar sign.
- **Sold out tonight?** Untick **Available**. The dish stays on the menu, greyed out, so guests know
  it exists and is off. Tick it back tomorrow.
- **Price changes with the market?** Leave the price blank and it shows "Ask your server".

Brand-new dish, or a whole new section? That needs your developer — it keeps the menu layout from
breaking.

> **Nine items on your menu currently have no price.** They read "Ask your server". Adding real
> prices is the single biggest improvement you can make to the website today.

---

## 6. Replace a photo

You can't do this from the admin — and that is on purpose. Each photo slot has a fixed shape so it
crops correctly on phones; uploading straight in would break those crops.

**Photos** shows every slot and which ones are still empty. Send the pictures to your developer and
they will drop them in.

**Shoot these first — there is currently no photograph of any dish on the website:**

1. Quesabirrias with the consommé
2. The Bizza
3. Birria ramen
4. A Friday or Saturday night on the floor

**A phone is fine.** Hold it **vertically**, stand near a window in the morning, turn the overhead
lights off, and fill the frame with the plate. Send the original file — don't post it to Instagram
first, that squashes the quality.

---

## 7. Read and reply to enquiries

**Enquiries** — catering requests, private-event requests, and job applications all land here.

Click the email or phone number to reply. Set the status to **Working on it**, then **Done**. Add
notes only your team sees.

> ⚠️ **Nobody is emailed automatically.** Check this page every day. The website tells guests
> exactly that when they send a message — it never pretends you have been emailed.

---

## 8. Preview and publish

There is no separate "publish" step. **Save it and it is live**, usually within a minute.

Every editing page has a **Preview on the website** link so you can check.

Not showing up? Wait a minute and refresh. Still missing after five, call your developer.

## Who to call

| Something | Who |
|---|---|
| Website looks broken | Your developer, immediately |
| Locked out of the admin | Another owner or manager |
| Reservations or online ordering broken | Toast support — those are Toast's systems, not this website |
| Need a new dish, a new page, or a photo swapped | Your developer |
| Everything else | Try it in the admin. Nothing there can break the website's appearance. |
